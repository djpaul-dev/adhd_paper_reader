Focus Reader

A calmer way to get through a research paper — one chunk at a time.

  1. Open a PDF. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; it stays in your browser.
  2. Set one goal. "What do I actually need from this paper?" Keeps you anchored.
  3. Start a focus block. 25 minutes, then a real break. Repeat.
  4. Read one sentence at a time. The paper keeps its original layout; a spotlight frames just the current sentence and dims the rest. → and ← move you; Space lets it advance on its own.
  5. Switch to Clean text for a reflowed single column with bionic reading, if the layout gets in the way.
  6. Park distractions. Thought pops up? Dump it in the parking lot and keep reading.
